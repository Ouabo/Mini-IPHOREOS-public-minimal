# O1-O4 overview video: toy-scale evidence for the grant's own objectives

**Video:** [O1_O4_Overview_EN.mp4](O1_O4_Overview_EN.mp4)

Duration: 3 minutes 15 seconds. Resolution: 1280 x 720 at 12 frames per second. English on-screen explanations; no voice-over or audio. A companion, cross-cutting video: where [EXT1_Explanation_EN.mp4](EXT1_Explanation_EN.mp4), [EXT1_Memory_Explanation_EN.mp4](EXT1_Memory_Explanation_EN.mp4) and [EXT1_BiasHeldout_Explanation_EN.mp4](EXT1_BiasHeldout_Explanation_EN.mp4) each walk through one milestone in order, this one re-reads the project's existing results against the EIC grant's own four objectives (O1-O4), as stated in `EIC_Objectives_Methodology/IPHOREOS_Section_1_3_Objectives_and_Methodology_Final3.docx`.

**What this video is not.** O1-O4 describe the grant's eventual TRL4 demonstrator: two real task contexts (shelf retrieval and component sorting/placement), on physical hardware. Mini-IPHOREOS's wall-approach robot is a small, desktop, single-task precursor. This video does not claim the toy satisfies O1-O4 -- it states, per objective, what toy-scale preliminary evidence already exists, and what each piece of evidence does not establish. Every chapter's caveat line is load-bearing, not boilerplate.

## What the video shows

- 00:00-00:10: title card, framing the scope limit above before any objective is discussed.
- 00:10-00:50 (**O1**, "Learn grounded concepts and events"): the joint distance/speed belief on the `normal` scenario, its uncertainty band tracked live. Toy evidence: calibrated uncertainty throughout (not a bare point estimate), the explicit `concept_target`/`realised_event` log vocabulary, and Milestone 4's frozen held-out replication (memory+fast reconvergence 3.3 vs 4.7 on development seeds, 3.2 vs 4.6 on held-out seeds 50000-50099, never touched during tuning). Caveat: no entities/relations generalising beyond training appearances, no matched unstructured-representation baseline, at this scale.
- 00:50-01:35 (**O2**, "Infer context and allocate computation"): the `ext1_memory_fast` policy on the `switch` scenario, context probability swinging toward the slow hypothesis. Toy evidence: a compact two-context mixture that concentrates belief mass on the active hypothesis without discarding the other (it can still be recovered), a change-point detector deciding when to re-weight, and the planner's cost-weighted duration menu. Caveat: no hardware-level latency, memory or energy measurement -- only logical decision counts and simulated `compute_ms`.
- 01:35-02:25 (**O3**, "Recompose knowledge for adaptive planning"): side by side, cold-start vs. zero-update-transfer on Task B (seed 10000, the case closest to the published paired-mean effect, not a cherry-picked best case). This is the Transfer system's actual, already-published result, not a new claim: post-switch reconvergence 5.20 -> 3.79-3.85 decisions, paired over 100 deployments (61 faster, 32 tied, 7 slower), while overall success/moves/time-to-goal stay statistically unchanged -- both halves shown together.
- 02:25-03:05 (**O4**, "Establish bounded trustworthiness"): the `outlier` scenario, a corrupted-but-present reading rejected by the innovation gate, visibly flagged as `unexpected_outcome` in the decision record. Toy evidence: an uncertainty-aware verification rule, the planner's hard overshoot-margin exclusion (a sequence that could overshoot is removed from consideration outright, not merely cost-penalised), and the full per-decision trace shown on screen as a faithful, after-the-fact-unedited audit trail. Caveat, stated without softening: hardware testing is **not** satisfied -- no physical robot has been built or run.
- 03:05-03:15: closing status card, one line per objective, restating the TRL4-demonstrator gap.

## Evidence and interpretation

Four of five traces are read directly from `results_ext1/` (`trace_normal_ext1.json`, `trace_switch_ext1_memory_fast.json`, `trace_outlier_ext1.json`; seed 10000, generated by `experiments/run_simulation.py`), unmodified. The O3 chapter uses two new traces generated by this video's preparation step, not by `experiments/run_transfer_validation.py` itself (which records aggregate statistics, not full per-decision logs): `results_transfer/trace_switch_cold_start_seed10000.json` and `results_transfer/trace_switch_zero_update_transfer_seed10000.json`, produced by driving `run_transfer_validation.py`'s own `build_checkpoint`/`make_task_b_controller`/`episode` functions directly for seed 10000 (deployment_factor ~0.808). Seed 10000 was chosen because its single-trial reconvergence gap (5 vs 4 decisions) is the closest available match to the published 100-deployment paired-mean effect (1.41 decisions) -- it is the representative case, not the most favourable one in the batch.

Every number spoken on screen (the O1 held-out figures, the O2 description of the mixture/CUSUM mechanism, the O3 paired statistics, the O4 verification-rule and overshoot-margin description) is read from an already-published result -- `Mathematical_Model_EXT1.pdf` (Sections 9-10) and `Tutorial_EXT1_EN.pdf` (Sections 6-7, 11) -- not computed fresh for this video. No new simulation, no new objective-to-evidence mapping decision, and no new number appears here that is not already on the record elsewhere.

No physical robot data or hardware validation is presented anywhere in this video. The O4 chapter's hardware caveat is the sharpest point in the whole video: everything else here is preliminary, toy-scale, desktop-simulation evidence, and is presented as exactly that.

## Files and reproducibility

`manifest_o1o4_EN.json` records the source file hashes (`cognitive/controller.py`, `cognitive/model.py`, `cognitive/baselines.py`, `experiments/run_transfer_validation.py`) and which trace file backs each panel. `preview_o1o4_EN_5s.png` through `_190s.png` are still previews, one per chapter plus the closing card.

To rebuild, from the `Mini_IPHOREOS_EXT1` directory: `python tools/make_o1_o4_video.py`. Requires Pillow and imageio-ffmpeg (see `requirements.txt`), a populated `results_ext1/` (run `experiments/run_simulation.py` first if missing), and the two O3 trace files in `results_transfer/` noted above (regenerate by re-running the snippet recorded in this video's preparation, driving `experiments/run_transfer_validation.py`'s `build_checkpoint`/`make_task_b_controller`/`episode` for seed 10000 and saving each condition's returned `logs`).
